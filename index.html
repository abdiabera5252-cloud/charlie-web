Botanical Provenance & Lore

Know Your Seeds: The Seed Encyclopedia

Unlike glass or plastic beads, every seed in our studio has a living botanical story. Explore the exact seeds harvested from forests, riverbanks, and tropical grasses to create your jewelry.

From Forest Floor to Studio

Slow-Crafted by Hand, Never Mass-Produced

Each seed is ethically gathered after falling naturally from trees and grasses. We sun-cure, hand-sort for symmetry, micro-drill with precision, and finish every piece with hypoallergenic hooks and durable cords.

1 Sustainably gathered fallen seeds & pods
2 Sun-dried & buffed with natural beeswax/plant oil
3 Paired by natural weight for featherlight wear

100% Natural Colors

The vivid scarlet of Abrus, pearly grey of Job’s Tears, and deep espresso of Entada beans are completely natural—zero paints or chemical stains.

Featherlight All-Day Comfort

Even bold statement earrings and multi-strand necklaces feel remarkably light, preventing earlobe strain while making an unmistakable statement.

One-of-a-Kind Variations

No two seeds grow identically. Subtle organic curves, natural furrows, and warm tonal shifts ensure your jewelry is uniquely yours.

Heirloom Seed Care

Keep away from prolonged water immersion. Occasionally buff matte seeds with a drop of coconut or jojoba oil to enrich their natural luster.
